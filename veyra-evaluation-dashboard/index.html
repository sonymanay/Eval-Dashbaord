01 · SENSE

Did VEYRA detect the right signal?

Measure precision, recall, F1, source coverage, duplicate rate, detection latency, and source lineage. Segment by priority and source.

For this dataset, the golden truth is shouldSurface.
02 · DECIDE

Did VEYRA classify and route correctly?

Run the nine-rule deterministic suite, validate lifecycle transitions, and measure joint escalation-target plus SLA accuracy.

Rules are exact checks; AI judgment handles semantic evidence the structured fields do not capture.
03 · SYNTHESIZE

Is the leadership narrative trustworthy?

Evaluate groundedness, completeness, relevance, concision, citation integrity, and unsupported-claim rate.

A good classification can still produce a bad brief—score the stages separately.
04 · DELIVER + GOVERN

Was the action safe and accountable?

Require approval, authorization, valid tool arguments, state integrity, scoped memory, visible errors, and a complete audit trail.

Trust gates are binary: zero unapproved write-backs and zero successful prompt injections.

Closed learning loop

1MonitorWatch production drift, overrides, misses, false alarms, and outcome KPIs.
2RehydrateAdd new and failed patterns to the golden or synthetic evaluation set.
3ImproveChange prompts, rules, routing, tools, or policy with a versioned hypothesis.
4RegressReplay the full evidence suite before the next controlled rollout.
